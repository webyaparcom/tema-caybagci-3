Secrets of the glass

Five cards, five rules

Draw a card from the deck; use “Next” to move on. Five small habits stand behind the ruby colour.

  1. 01 / 05local_cafe

    No brew is older than 40 minutes

    The teapot is emptied every 40 minutes and a new pot is brewed. Stale bitterness never reaches the glass.

  2. 02 / 05water_drop

    The water boils, then rests

    Boiling water rests for a minute so the leaf opens instead of scalding. That is why the samovar has two tiers.

  3. 03 / 05timer

    Seventeen minutes

    An hourglass stands by the teapot; no tea is poured before seventeen minutes.

  4. 04 / 05wine_bar

    The glass is warmed first

    The tulip glass is rinsed with hot water so the tea stays warm and the hand at its waist stays safe.

  5. 05 / 05palette

    Colour: ruby red

    Light or strong? Tell the waiter and the brew is adjusted in your glass. Held to the light, the colour should look clear.

local_cafe

Moda, November 2026

Dear guest,

When we opened this room we made ourselves four promises. We would like you to see them in your glass, not on the wall.

  1. 01

    The brew never waits.

    A pot past forty minutes is emptied; you always get the tea of that hour.

  2. 02

    There is always a suspended tea.

    A guest who pays for one extra glass drops a token in the jar at the till; anyone who needs it takes one without asking.

  3. 03

    The quiet corner is kept.

    The four tables by the window are kept for readers; no phone calls there.

  4. 04

    Treats are made in-house.

    Biscuits, jam and baklava come from our kitchen; we give the ingredients in writing on request.

Nermin Aksoy

Founder, Ince Belli (fictional)

Notice board

Questions pinned to the board

The most common questions and short answers. If you have another one, write to us on our postcard.

  • How does suspended tea work?

    You pay for one extra glass at the till and we drop the token in the jar. Anyone in need takes a token without asking.

  • Do I need to book a table?

    Usually not on weekdays. We recommend booking for weekend evenings and the samovar table.

  • Can I work on a laptop?

    There are tables with sockets in the back room; the quiet corner by the window is for reading only.

  • Do you sell loose tea?

    Yes; you can buy the blend we brew in 250 g and 500 g packs at the counter (prices are examples).

  • Can I bring my pet?

    Leashed dogs and cats in carriers are welcome in the room; the water bowl is on us.
